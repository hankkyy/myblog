Silicon Valley has already laid off more than 300,000 people in 2024-2025.

## Big Tech Layoff Timeline

- Google: 12,000 layoffs in 2024, another 8,000 in 2025
- Meta: 10,000 layoffs in 2024 (Year of Efficiency)
- Amazon: Layoffs across both AWS and retail, totaling 25,000+
- Microsoft: 12,000+ layoffs in Azure and gaming divisions

## Surface-Level Causes

- Economic downturn, declining ad revenue
- Uncertain ROI on AI investments
- Overexpansion during the pandemic

## Deeper Structural Changes

1. **AI Automation Replacing Jobs**: Basic CRUD, testing, and operations work are being replaced by AI tools
2. **Impact of Remote Work**: Efficiency hasn't lived up to expectations, and communication costs have increased
3. **Shift in Investment Trends**: From "burn cash for growth" to "pursuing profitability"
4. **Global Division of Labor**: Engineers in India and Eastern Europe cost only 1/5 of their Silicon Valley counterparts

## Advice for Newcomers to the Tech Industry

- Don't just focus on big tech companies
- Build technical depth rather than breadth
- Pay attention to growth areas like AI and data infrastructure
- Accumulate open-source contributions and hands-on project experience

The winter will eventually pass, but the industry's demands on talent will only get higher.
