OpenAI has released Sora 2, taking video generation quality to the next level.

## Improvements

- Generates videos up to 10 minutes long (Sora 1 was limited to 1 minute)
- Resolution: 4K
- Frame rate: stable 30fps
- Supports multi-shot transitions and camera movement control
- Can specify "styles" — cinematic, documentary, vlog, advertising

## Real-World Results

I watched several demos, and they're truly impressive. A 30-second "futuristic city" commercial-grade video was generated in under 2 minutes. While there are still some unnatural details upon closer inspection (such as character fingers and lighting changes), it's already good enough for commercial use.

## Industry Impact

- Short-form video creators: production costs drop significantly
- Advertising agencies: high-quality ads achievable on small budgets
- Film and TV industry: effects and backgrounds can be generated directly
- Stock footage sites: AI video may replace live-action footage

Interestingly, Adobe is an OpenAI partner, and Sora 2 will be integrated into Premiere Pro.

The barrier to content creation keeps getting lower — going forward, it's no longer about technical skill but creativity and aesthetic taste.
