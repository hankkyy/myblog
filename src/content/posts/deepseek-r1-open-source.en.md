The biggest AI news in early 2025 came from China—DeepSeek R1 went open source.

## What is R1

DeepSeek R1 is a reasoning-focused model, positioned as a rival to OpenAI's o1. Key features:
- Fully open source (both weights and training methods are public)
- Extremely low training cost (reportedly only a few million dollars)
- Near o1-level performance on benchmarks like MATH and AIME
- Supports Chain-of-Thought reasoning

## Why it caused a stir

Previously, the consensus was that "top-tier AI requires astronomical investment." DeepSeek proved that with innovative training methods, a good model can be built at relatively low cost.

This directly led to a 5% drop in Nvidia's stock price that day—if you can do AI without stacking GPUs, Nvidia's high-growth story takes a hit.

## Impact on the industry

- The open-source community exploded: R1's downloads on Hugging Face surpassed one million within a week
- A domestic AI startup boom: ByteDance, Alibaba, and Baidu all accelerated their efforts
- Silicon Valley introspection: US chip export controls on China actually forced better engineering innovation

DeepSeek's emergence illustrates a key point: in the AI race, algorithmic innovation may matter more than raw compute.
