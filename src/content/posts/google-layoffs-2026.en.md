Google is laying off employees again.

This time, it's the Google Cloud division, cutting 15% — roughly 7,000 people. Pichai was blunt in his internal memo: AI automation can already replace many traditional roles.

## Which roles are most affected?

- Site Reliability Engineers (SRE): automated operations tools have matured enough
- Junior developers: AI tools like Copilot/Cody have doubled the efficiency of senior engineers
- Some mid-level management: flattening + AI-assisted decision-making

## Trend assessment

The wave of layoffs at major US tech companies won't stop. At its core, this is a structural shift in talent demand driven by AI productivity gains. Work that used to require 10 people can now be done by 5 people plus AI.

For newcomers, the old path of "grinding LeetCode to get into a big tech company" is getting increasingly difficult. You need real technical depth, not just CRUD skills.
