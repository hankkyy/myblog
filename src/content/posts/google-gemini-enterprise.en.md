Google has announced the deep integration of Gemini into the entire Workspace suite.

## New Features

- **Docs**: Say "help me write a Q4 sales report," and Gemini automatically pulls data from Sheets to generate a document with charts
- **Sheets**: Say "analyze this quarter's trends," and it automatically generates pivot tables and visualizations
- **Gmail**: Can automatically categorize, summarize, and draft replies, and even create calendar events based on email content
- **Slides**: Say "make a 10-page product introduction deck," and it generates one automatically

## How It Differs from Microsoft Copilot

- Google's data is more "native"—Gmail, Docs, and Sheets data live in the same ecosystem, so the AI can draw on data across products
- Microsoft's advantage is Office's dominance in the enterprise market, and Copilot's Excel features are stronger than Sheets'

## Pricing

Gemini for Workspace costs $30 per user per month, the same price as Microsoft Copilot. Google has also launched a free version for students.
