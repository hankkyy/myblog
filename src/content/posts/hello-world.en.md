This is my first blog post.

## Why I Write a Blog

Writing a blog is the best way to organize your thoughts. Only by putting your ideas down in writing do you truly understand what you're thinking.

## How This Blog Was Built

I used Python to write a simple static site generator and deployed it on Vercel. The design draws inspiration from the Cenote theme on WordPress—clean, pure, and focused on the text.

### Tech Stack

- **Python**: Custom build scripts that parse Markdown and generate HTML
- **Vercel**: Deployment and hosting, with built-in CDN and HTTPS
- **Fonts**: Roboto (body) + Catamaran (headings), from Google Fonts

> A good blog doesn't need fancy design.
> White space is the greatest respect you can show your readers.
