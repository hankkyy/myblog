Tesla showcased the latest progress of its Optimus robot at AI Day.

## What It Can Do Now

- Moving materials in the factory (battery packs, components)
- Simple assembly tasks (screwing, plugging in connectors)
- Warehouse sorting and palletizing
- Autonomous navigation and obstacle avoidance

These are almost all repetitive manual labor tasks, and its precision and speed still fall short of experienced workers.

## Technical Details

- Height: 173 cm, Weight: 73 kg
- 40 joint degrees of freedom
- Equipped with the same AI chip as FSD
- Battery life: 8 hours
- Walking speed: 5 km/h (roughly the same as a human walking)

## Mass Production Plans

Musk said external sales will begin in 2026, priced at $20,000-$30,000. However, industry insiders generally believe this timeline is overly optimistic.

## Significance

Regardless of whether the timeline holds up, Tesla is currently the company investing the most in the humanoid robot space. If Optimus can truly achieve a $20,000 price point and become practical, manufacturing will undergo a seismic shift.

Of course, Musk's timelines should always be taken with a grain of salt.
