Apple has released the Vision Pro 2, which is significantly lighter than the first generation and comes at a lower price.

## Improvements

- Weight: 350g (first generation was 600g+)
- Price: $2499 (first generation was $3499)
- Chip: M4
- Battery life: 4 hours (first generation was 2.5 hours)
- Wearing method: No external battery pack needed

## But the Problems Remain

1. **Lack of killer apps**: Besides watching videos and giving presentations, everyday use cases are limited
2. **Social awkwardness**: Wearing such a large headset out in public still feels odd
3. **Developers haven't caught up**: There are still very few apps specifically built for Vision Pro

## My Take

The Vision Pro's hardware is indeed impressive, but the concept of "spatial computing" hasn't been embraced by the mainstream yet. It may take until Vision Pro 3 or 4 to see a real market breakthrough.

Interestingly, the Meta Quest is actually selling quite well — because it's affordable ($499) and has a mature gaming ecosystem. Apple's premium approach may not be the right fit for this product category.
