Temu's expansion pace in 2025 has been incredibly aggressive.

From its US launch in September 2022 to now, it has entered 80 countries in just over three years. In 2025, global GMV surpassed $40 billion.

## Why It Succeeds

1. **Extreme Low Prices**: For the same products, Temu is 50-80% cheaper than Amazon
2. **Gamified Shopping**: Price-slashing, lucky wheels—shopping feels like playing a game
3. **Fully Managed Model**: Merchants only handle supply; Temu manages operations, logistics, and after-sales
4. **Advertising Blitz**: In 2024, Temu spent over $3 billion on advertising across Meta and Google

## Controversies

- Inconsistent product quality
- Environmental concerns (excessive packaging + short life cycles of cheap goods)
- Data security scrutiny (facing similar pressure in the US as TikTok)
- Impact on local small and medium-sized merchants

## Competitors

- Shein: Stronger in the apparel category
- Amazon: Launched its low-cost channel Amazon Haul to directly compete
- Shopee/Lazada: Competing head-on with Temu in Southeast Asia

Pinduoduo's overseas strategy is hard not to admire. But whether low prices are truly a sustainable business model still needs time to prove.
