Baidu has released ERNIE 5.0.

## Key Improvements

- Chinese comprehension reportedly surpasses GPT-4 Turbo
- Supports ultra-long context (500K tokens, capable of processing the entire "Three-Body Problem" in one go)
- Multimodal: supports image and audio input
- Deep integration with Baidu Search (real-time data is an exclusive advantage)

## Competitors

Baidu was the earliest mover in China's AI space, but competitors are catching up quickly:
- ByteDance (Doubao): user numbers have already surpassed ERNIE Bot
- Alibaba (Tongyi Qianwen): leveraging the Alibaba Cloud ecosystem
- Tencent (Hunyuan): WeChat 9.0's AI assistant is gaining momentum
- DeepSeek: the open-source champion of cost-effectiveness

## Baidu's Advantage

The search engine (with billions of queries daily) serves as a natural data flywheel. ERNIE does have a genuine edge in knowledge Q&A and real-time information retrieval.

But the issue is that competition in the LLM space has shifted from "who's stronger" to "who's cheaper" and "who has the better ecosystem." DeepSeek is open-source and free, while Doubao taps into Douyin/Toutiao's traffic pool—Baidu doesn't hold the advantage in ecosystem.

Robin Li said, "AI is Baidu's second startup." Time will tell whether that promise holds up.
