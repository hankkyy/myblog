WeChat's mini program platform has launched an AI Agent feature.

## How to Use It

Mini program developers can integrate AI capabilities into their mini programs through the API provided by WeChat:
- Intelligent customer service (automatically answering user questions)
- Product recommendations (recommending based on browsing history)
- Content generation (automatically generating product descriptions and marketing copy)
- Voice interaction (voice ordering, voice search)

## Differences from OpenAI GPTs

WeChat's AI Agent places more emphasis on "scenario-based" applications:
- Not general-purpose conversation, but vertical scenarios (e-commerce, food and beverage, education, etc.)
- Deep integration with WeChat Pay and WeChat Login
- Stricter review process (after all, it's within the WeChat ecosystem)

## What It Means for Small Merchants

Previously, only large companies could build intelligent customer service and personalized recommendation systems. Now, even a small street-side shop can integrate an AI customer service bot with just a few dozen lines of code.

The mini program ecosystem is already mature (with 600 million+ daily active users). With the addition of AI capabilities, we may see a wave of "AI-native WeChat mini programs" emerge.

WeChat's moat just keeps getting wider.
