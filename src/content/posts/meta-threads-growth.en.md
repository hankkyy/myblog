Threads has surpassed 500 million monthly active users, officially overtaking Musk's X.

## Growth Trajectory

- Launched in July 2023, hit 100 million in 5 days (powered by Instagram integration)
- Stabilized at 200 million monthly active users in 2024
- Accelerated growth in 2025 after opening up the EU market
- Surpassed 500 million in January 2026

## Key Differences from X

- Threads follows a "positive social" approach, avoiding politically polarizing content recommendations
- X has increasingly become a "free speech experiment" under Musk's leadership
- Threads' monetization is more restrained, with minimal advertising
- X has introduced paid subscriptions, creator revenue sharing, and other monetization tools

## Appeal to Users

Many people left X not because of technical issues, but because of the atmosphere. Threads' product is simpler (perhaps even too simple—it doesn't even have hashtags), but it's "less noisy."

## Meta's Social Empire

Facebook (3 billion) + Instagram (2 billion) + WhatsApp (2 billion) + Threads (500 million) + Messenger—Meta's dominance in the social space remains undiminished.

Zuckerberg played this one beautifully.
