Amazon CEO Andy Jassy sent a company-wide email: starting July 2026, all employees must work from the office five days a week.

## Background

Amazon began requiring three days in the office in 2023, upgraded to four days in 2024, and now it's straight to five. Jassy's stated reasons:

- In-person collaboration is more efficient
- New hire onboarding requires in-person guidance
- Innovation capabilities decline in remote environments

## Employee Reaction

Internal Slack channels exploded. Many employees say this is effectively a disguised layoff—with high housing prices in Seattle/SF Bay Area plus commuting costs, a significant number may choose to leave.

A previous internal survey showed that over 70% of employees wanted to maintain a hybrid work model.

## Industry Trends

- Google: Three days a week, no changes for now
- Meta: Three days a week, Mark says "remote work is highly efficient"
- Apple: Three days a week, Cook is more hardline
- Microsoft: Flexible work, left to each team's discretion

Amazon's move could be an isolated case, or it could be a bellwether for the entire industry. The golden age of remote work may indeed be over.
