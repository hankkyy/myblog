It's been a full year since Sam Altman returned to OpenAI at the end of 2024. Looking back at the past year, OpenAI has changed enormously.

## Restructuring

OpenAI announced its transition to a for-profit company (PBC, Public Benefit Corporation). This means:
- No longer constrained by the non-profit board
- Can raise funds and go public normally
- But it has also sparked much controversy—Musk filed a lawsuit claiming this betrays OpenAI's original mission

## Talent Flow

Key people who left OpenAI over the past year:
- Ilya Sutskever (co-founder, went to Safe Superintelligence)
- Mira Murati (former CTO)
- Multiple safety team members

Altman's take is that "this is normal talent turnover," but outsiders interpret it as a clash between the safety faction and the commercial faction.

## Products

- ChatGPT monthly active users grew from 100 million to 300 million
- GPT-4 Turbo and GPT-4o released
- Sora video generation model launched
- Partnership with Apple (Apple Intelligence integrates ChatGPT)

OpenAI is no longer that "non-profit research lab"—it is becoming another tech giant.
